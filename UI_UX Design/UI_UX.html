Egusi Soup with Pounded Yam

4.7 (94 reviews) 60 min Serves 6 Medium

A rich, velvety soup made from ground melon seeds, leafy greens, assorted meats and fish. Served with pounded yam, it's the centrepiece of countless Nigerian Sunday tables.

  • Ingredients
  • Instructions
  • Video
  • Reviews (94)
2 cupsEgusi (melon) seeds, ground
500 gAssorted meat (beef, tripe, shaki)
300 gDried fish or stockfish, soaked
1 cupPalm oil
4 pcsScotch bonnet peppers, blended
1 bunchUgu (pumpkin) or spinach leaves
2 tbspGround crayfish
2 cubesSeasoning (Maggi / Knorr)
1 tspSalt, to taste
2 tbspLocust beans (Iru) — optional
1
Cook the meats. Season assorted meats with onion, salt and a seasoning cube. Boil for 20 minutes until tender. Reserve the stock.
2
Prepare the egusi paste. Mix ground egusi with a little water to form a thick paste.
3
Fry the base. Heat palm oil, add sliced onions, then the egusi paste in lumps. Fry for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4
Build the soup. Add blended pepper, reserved stock, locust beans and crayfish. Simmer for 15 minutes.
5
Finish. Stir in cooked meats, dried fish, and finally the ugu leaves. Simmer for 5 more minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning.
6
Serve hot with pounded yam, eba or fufu.

OUR STORY

Preserving Nigerian flavours, one recipe at a time.

NaijaCipe was built by three students who missed home cooking. What started as a shared Google Doc of family recipes is now a platform for anyone who wants to cook Nigerian.

🎯

Our mission

Make authentic Nigerian recipes accessible and searchable, with enough detail that a first-time cook can succeed.

🌍

Who it's for

Diaspora cooks, curious food lovers, and anyone who wants to cook Nigerian beyond Jollof Rice.

⚙️

Built with

Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML/CSS, Bootstrap 5. Everything you're learning in EFSSD.
